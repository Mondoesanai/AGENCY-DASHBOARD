Privacy — text messages

The sentence that matters

We do not sell or share your mobile number, and we do not share it with anyone for their marketing. It is used to text you about your preview and nothing else.

What we keep

  • Your mobile number.
  • Your name, if you gave one.
  • A record of how and when you opted in — which page or keyword, the exact wording you agreed to, and the version of it.
  • The messages between us.

We keep the opt-in record because we have to be able to show that you agreed, and because you are entitled to see it too.

Who else sees it

Our messaging provider carries the messages, and the mobile carriers deliver them. Nobody else receives your number from us.

Stopping, and being forgotten

Reply STOP and we stop immediately. If you ask us to erase your details we will — but we keep the record that the number opted out, because that record is the thing that stops us contacting you again.
