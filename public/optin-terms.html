Text message terms

These terms cover the text messages Inspiring Websites sends about free website previews.

What you are agreeing to

If you opt in, we may text you about your free website preview and send occasional updates about our service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How you opt in

  • Text PREVIEW to our number, or
  • enter your number on our opt-in page and then text PREVIEW from that phone to confirm it is yours.

We do not text a number until a message has arrived from it. Entering a number on the form alone does not sign anyone up — this is deliberate, so that nobody can sign up somebody else.

How you stop

Reply STOP to any message and we stop immediately and permanently. Reply HELP for help, or email the address on our website.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Changes

If these terms change, the change applies to messages sent after it. What you agreed to is recorded with the version of the wording that was published when you opted in.
